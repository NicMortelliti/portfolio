I've always been curious about how things work. That curiosity has taken me through a career in aviation and systems engineering, into software development, and plenty of projects along the way.

Outside of work, I enjoy traveling, exploring new places, and documenting some of those experiences through photography. I've even learned to develop my own film, which offers a different kind of satisfaction from the digital world I spend most of my time in.

I'm also a pilot, although I don't get up in the air nearly as often these days. These days, I'd rather spend that time with my family. The fascination with aviation hasn't gone anywhere, though.

I like making things, learning new skills, and staying curious. Software engineering is a big part of my life, but it's certainly not the whole story.
