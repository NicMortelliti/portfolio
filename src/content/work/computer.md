I've always enjoyed the quirky computer character from The LEGO Movie, so I decided to recreate it as a little side project.

It's a deliberately simple recreation, with blinking eyes and animated dots and sliders that give the character a bit of life. It doesn't serve much of a practical purpose, but that was never really the point.

Just a fun excuse to build something, experiment with animation, and bring a character I liked into the digital world.
