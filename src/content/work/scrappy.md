Scrappy is a personal project I'm building to create a better way to preserve the experiences and little moments that make up a life.

Part travel journal, part digital scrapbook, and part family memory archive, the idea is to make it easy to capture moments as they happen and bring them together into a story later.

Built with Ruby on Rails, Hotwire, and Turbo, Scrappy is designed around trips and moments, with support for bringing together written notes and photos, videos, and other media in a chronological timeline.

It's an exploration of building software around something personal: spending less time organizing memories and more time making them.
