At Collins Aerospace, I worked on Pro Line Fusion avionics programs as a Senior Systems and Human Factors Engineer, serving as the engineering focal for several cockpit graphical user interfaces.

My responsibilities included Engine Instrumentation, Crew Alerting, Synoptic displays, and Electronic Checklists. I developed ARINC 661 GUI prototypes, contributed to human factors requirements, and supported interface development across multiple avionics programs.

I also mentored engineers on GUI development methods, helping promote consistency across the development process.

This work brought together systems engineering, software interface development, and human factors principles to support intuitive cockpit interfaces for pilots operating complex aircraft.
