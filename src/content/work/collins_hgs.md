At Collins Aerospace, I worked as a Senior Systems and Human Factors Engineer on head-up guidance and enhanced vision system programs for Gulfstream and Boeing 737 aircraft.

For the Gulfstream HGS program, I served as verification lead, achieving full requirements verification coverage. On the Boeing 737 NG Enhanced Vision System program, I developed the Human Factors Flight Test Plan.

I also configured a fixed-base C/C++ simulator to emulate verification-aircraft avionics, developed a command-line tool to control video sources and visual characteristics for HUD Enhanced Video System demonstrations, and performed simulator safety-of-flight checkouts.

My experience as a pilot brought an operational perspective to the work, connecting system behavior and verification activities with the needs of the people using these systems in the cockpit.
