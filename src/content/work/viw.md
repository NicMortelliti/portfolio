At Parry Labs, I was the sole architect and developer responsible for the VIW web application, a cloud-based workspace that enables teams to configure and work with digital twins of mission systems before deployment.

I owned the application end to end, from architectural decisions and technology selection to implementation, testing, and delivery. I also led its evolution from a React frontend and Rails API into an integrated Rails application built with Hotwire and Turbo.

The work brought together my background in systems engineering and software development, solving complex problems while building a practical tool for virtual integration and testing.
